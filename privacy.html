Ten Up Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Ten Up does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your iPhone

Ten Up keeps your game in progress and your game settings (number of opponents, difficulty and game length) on your iPhone, so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device and the developer can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, that backup may include Ten Up's saved game, the same as for other apps. Apple handles those backups under your Apple Account settings.

What Ten Up doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No internet connection. The app works fully offline and doesn't send data anywhere.
  • No ads, analytics or crash reporting services
  • No third-party code that collects data
  • No tracking across other apps or websites

Children

Ten Up doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

App Store

Apple, not the developer, handles your download and purchase of Ten Up under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
