Ten Up Support

Ten Up is a rummy-style card game for iPhone. You work through ten phases against computer opponents.

Questions, problems or suggestions? Send an email to

info@nelsoncomputers.com

If you're reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened just before it.

Common questions

How do I learn the rules?

The full rules are in the app. During a game, tap the book icon at the top of the screen. If you aren't sure what to lay down, tap Find Phase with no cards selected and it highlights a phase you can play.

Is my game saved?

Yes. Your game saves automatically on your iPhone, so you can close the app and pick up where you left off. Deleting the app also deletes the saved game.

Can I play with friends online?

No. Ten Up is a single-player game against 1 to 5 computer opponents, at Easy, Normal or Hard difficulty.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Ten Up works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
