Common questions.

How do I learn the rules?

During a game, tap the book icon at the top of the screen. Not sure what to lay down? Tap Find Phase with no cards selected to see a phase you can play.

Is my game saved?

Yes. Ten Up saves automatically, so you can close the app and pick up where you left off. Deleting the app also deletes the saved game.

Can I play with friends?

Ten Up is single-player. You play against 1 to 5 computer opponents at Easy, Normal or Hard difficulty.

Do I need internet?

No. Ten Up works fully offline.

Ads or purchases?

None. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Which devices?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
